# Achievement Ideas

## Milestone Counts
1. **First Blood** — Record your first puff
2. **Century** — 100 total puffs
3. **Kilo Chuffer** — 1,000 total puffs
4. **Ten Grand** — 10,000 total puffs
5. **Chronic** — 100,000 total puffs

## Duration Records
6. **Quick Draw** — A puff under 0.5 seconds
7. **Savour It** — A single puff lasting over 10 seconds
8. **Iron Lungs** — A single puff lasting over 20 seconds
9. **Are You Okay?** — A single puff lasting over 30 seconds
10. **Micro-Hit** — 10 puffs under 1 second in a row

## Frequency & Rate
11. **Chain Smoker** — 10 puffs within 2 minutes
12. **Gatling Gun** — 20 puffs within 5 minutes
13. **One and Done** — Only a single puff in an entire day
14. **Marathon Session** — At least one puff every 5 minutes for an hour straight
15. **Pace Yourself** — Exactly 1 puff per minute for 10 consecutive minutes (±5s tolerance)

## Time of Day
16. **Early Bird** — Puff before 6:00 AM
17. **Night Owl** — Puff after 2:00 AM
18. **Sunrise Rip** — Puff between 5:00–5:15 AM
19. **Lunch Break** — Puff between 12:00–12:05 PM every day for a week
20. **Around the Clock** — At least one puff in every hour of a 24-hour period

## Streaks & Consistency
21. **Daily Driver** — Puff on 7 consecutive days
22. **Monthly Pass** — Puff on 30 consecutive days
23. **Clean Streak** — No puffs for 24 hours (awarded to the device)
24. **Detox Week** — No puffs for 7 consecutive days
25. **Relapse** — First puff after a Clean Streak

## Dual Coil (Coil A + Coil B)
26. **Double Barrel** — Both coils active simultaneously
27. **Coil Loyalist** — 100 consecutive puffs on the same coil
28. **Ambidextrous** — Equal puffs on both coils within a day (±5%)
29. **Coil Swap** — Alternate between coil A and coil B 10 times in a row
30. **Favourite Child** — One coil has 10x the lifetime puffs of the other

## Multi-Device Interactions
31. **Synchronised Vaping** — Two devices puff within 1 second of each other
32. **Duet** — Two devices actively puffing at the exact same moment
33. **Mexican Wave** — 3+ devices puff in sequence within 10 seconds
34. **Copycat** — Device starts a puff within 3 seconds of another device stopping
35. **Rivalry** — Two devices each exceed 50 puffs in the same hour
36. **The Favourite** — One device has more puffs than all others combined
37. **Passing the Torch** — A new device's first puff happens within 1 minute of another device's last-ever puff
38. **Fleet Admiral** — 5+ devices registered simultaneously
39. **Lone Wolf** — Only one device active for 30 consecutive days
40. **Social Vaping** — 3+ devices all active within a 5-minute window

## Patterns & Oddities
41. **Morse Code** — A sequence of short-long-short puffs (<1s, >5s, <1s) within 30 seconds
42. **Metronome** — 5 puffs with identical intervals (±0.5s)
43. **Crescendo** — 5 consecutive puffs each longer than the last
44. **Decrescendo** — 5 consecutive puffs each shorter than the last
45. **Witching Hour** — Puff at exactly midnight (00:00:00 ±5s)
46. **New Year's Rip** — Puff in the first minute of January 1st
47. **Power Nap** — Device goes to deep sleep and wakes back up within 2 hours
48. **Phantom Puff** — A puff so short the firmware debounce almost caught it (exactly 0.5s)

## Cumulative / Lifetime
49. **Time Well Spent** — Total puff duration exceeds 1 hour
50. **Full Work Day** — Total puff duration exceeds 8 hours
51. **Dedicated** — Active across 3+ firmware versions (OTA updates tracked)
52. **Veteran Device** — A single device active for 365 days since first puff
